About Smart City

A Smart City uses modern technology and digital infrastructure to improve the quality of life of its citizens.

The city focuses on sustainable development, efficient public services, intelligent transportation and environmental protection.

Important: Smart City technologies help improve efficiency, safety, sustainability and citizen participation.

🌱 Green City

The city promotes environmental sustainability through renewable energy, green buildings and waste management.

  • Solar Energy
  • Green Buildings
  • Waste Recycling
  • Urban Parks

🚦 Smart Transportation

Intelligent transportation systems reduce traffic congestion and improve public mobility.

  • Smart Traffic Signals
  • Public Transport Tracking
  • Smart Parking
  • Electric Vehicles
